Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about AdminPalace

How does the one-step approval work?

Our AI-powered system pre-screens visitors against security databases and company policies. Once cleared, hosts receive instant notifications with visitor details and can approve with a single tap on their mobile device.

What security measures are in place?

We implement military-grade encryption, facial recognition, government ID verification, GDPR compliance, SOC 2 certification, and real-time threat detection to ensure maximum security.

Can it integrate with our existing systems?

Yes! AdminPalace integrates with 100+ enterprise tools including Slack, Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, access control systems, and calendar applications through our robust API.

Is it suitable for multiple locations?

Absolutely! Our multi-location management allows you to monitor and manage visitor access across all your offices globally from a single dashboard with location-specific policies.
